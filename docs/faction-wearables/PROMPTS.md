## Runtime exports

`tools/pack-faction-wearables.py` thresholds alpha, quantizes each authored view to sixteen colors, registers native body rectangles, and exports four tracked PNGs plus JSON contracts under `assets/art/sprites/player-wearables-{covenant,last-dawn}{,-icons}`. The two garment sheets are 256×960; icons are 192×32. This packing does not draw anatomy or change original player sheets.
