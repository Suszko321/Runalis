CLASS REPERTOIRE

Oathkeeper

Vowchain

control · 15 stamina · 6s cooldown

Cast an oath-chain. Pull lesser foes inward; against a massive foe, advance to the anchor.

Litany of Iron

channel · 6 spirit · 0s cooldown

Hold a planted stance. Frontal hits are reduced by 55%; pressure builds Conviction and retaliation.

Debt of Blood

defence · 14 stamina · 11s cooldown

Store part of suffered damage for five seconds, then release a close Judgment shockwave.

Mandate

control · 10 stamina · 7s cooldown

Condemn a foe for eight seconds. Three melee blows trigger a judgment strike.

CLASS REPERTOIRE

Fellhunter

Hunter’s Line

control · 12 stamina · 6s cooldown

Tether a foe for four seconds. Separation drags light foes or advances you toward heavy anchors and exposes the target.

Ricochet Blade

damage · 13 stamina · 4s cooldown

A heavy steel blade rebounds between three nearby foes, punishing Exposed targets.

Deadfall

area · 19 stamina · 10s cooldown

Arm a mechanical deadfall after a short delay. A nearby hostile triggers a crushing, staggering impact.

Execution Window

support · 12 stamina · 12s cooldown

For six seconds, weapon kills against Exposed or wounded foes partially recharge your oldest gadget, up to three times.

Vaulting Shot

mobility · 17 stamina · 7s cooldown

Vault backward from your aim while firing a steel shot at the threat. Terrain stops the vault.

CLASS REPERTOIRE

Gravecaller

Murmuring Host

channel · 5 spirit · 0s cooldown

Hold to gather a spectral congregation. Bound spirits seek foes in your aimed region. Kills briefly swell the Host.

Funeral Urn

swarm · 17 spirit · 7s cooldown

Throw a funerary vessel. After it shatters, four bound grave wisps seek nearby foes for three seconds.

Ghostwalk

mobility · 13 spirit · 6s cooldown

Glide through enemy bodies, leaving a spectral wake. Crossed enemies are Haunted and take stronger spirit hits.

Death Echo

summon · 12 spirit · 8s cooldown

Mark a living foe. If it dies within eight seconds, its temporary echo attacks former allies.

Seed Reliquary

swarm · 16 spirit · 7s cooldown

Throw a seed pod that splits into four short-lived biting growths. Each bite opens a Verdant Wound.

Thornstorm

channel · 6 spirit · 0s cooldown

Hold a rooted stance to erupt thorn volleys along your aim and slow nearby enemies.

Corpsebloom

summon · 14 spirit · 8s cooldown

Plant a death seed in a foe. Its death grows a temporary carnivorous flower that bites nearby enemies.

Tide Mirror
