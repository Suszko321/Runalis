Block

Hold to block incoming attacks, reducing their damage by 62% before class bonuses. Holding drains Stamina and slows movement; absorbed hits cost extra Stamina and can break your guard. Keeping Block on your hotbar grants 25% more Defence XP, even when you do not use it.
