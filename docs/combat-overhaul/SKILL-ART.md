# Every skill's icon and presentation

All 85 active skills now have a different authored primary silhouette. The older catalogue no longer falls back to shared class, weapon or letter icons. Class palettes unite the icon set; shapes distinguish daggers, jaws, hooks, seeds, vessels, blood lances, crowns, shield facets, beams and tears. SVG frames add a restrained bevel and shadow for readability. DOM HUDs, the ability library, the proving ground and the co-op canvas HUD use the same primary geometry. Legacy aliases resolve to their canonical skill's icon.

The 111 passive and weapon-tree nodes also have individual emblems. A large benefit symbol and a discipline badge communicate the passive's role. Named doctrines and later mastery stages receive distinct primary emblems. Active acquisition nodes show the actual active skill's icon. Passive skills retain passive behavior.

The original 56 abilities now have individual motion profiles rather than sharing only broad class profiles. Traps use ground placement; knives and vessels use throws; shield bashes drive a braced shield; volleys draw and release; healing lifts a restorative gesture; compression closes both hands; summoning and rifts use rising/opening gestures. North/south poses have separated hands, and every sampled pose recovers into its stance.

Release effects now select the skill's signature: steel fan, trap jaws, fuse sparks, bone ritual, blood lance, soul drain, thorn cage, rose jaws, tide crescent, pressure collapse, fountain, deluge crown, halo fragments, prismatic shield, sunrise, star collapse or toothed rift. Impacts use the actual damage source and remain brief. Additional mastery details stay at the periphery rather than implying a larger damaging area.

Sustained techniques received a further pass: different urn/seed vessels, ceramic shattering, spinning steel, narrow water ribbons, vortex foam, thorn eruptions along the clipped lane, distinct ground stances, branching conduits and dark angular portals. Blood Lance's original purple beam now uses a blood-colored core, spearhead and returning strands. Authoritative damage, hitboxes, costs, channel timing and terrain checks remain in the combat systems.

Open [the icon gallery](skill-art.html) to compare icons at 48, 24 and 16 pixels; selecting a card equips the skill in the save-free proving ground. The gallery includes a recorded showcase of twelve live casts. Shared/bloodline review skills set the required review bloodline only in that isolated page. The [combat catalogue](index.html) contains actual casts at three simulation times. The Combat Atelier also uses the current release renderer.

Validation includes all 85 actual cast checks, the 55 combat regressions, 182,196 finite animation samples and 3,400 browser atelier samples. The dedicated [art checks](skill-art-results.json) verify 85 unique active silhouettes, 85 individual motion IDs, 111 unique passive emblems, all tree asset references, and 6,120 nonblank effect renders across four directions, three mastery tiers and release/impact stages. Rendering leaves effect inputs and canvas settings unchanged. The [browser review](skill-art-browser-results.json) confirms 366 loaded icon previews, no compact gallery overflow, and ten successful direct-to-skill casts covering every class and the three bloodlines.

These are implemented procedural visuals in the established Runalis style. Screenshots and live casts were inspected; numerical checks do not certify subjective art quality. Authored directional sprite work, bespoke sound and further art-direction iterations remain possible refinements.
