The Sunken

Familiar Waverider magic drawn from deeper water. Lower opening damage gives way to stronger burning pressure against enemies that endure the fight.

OriginalSunken · Mastery 5Cryomancer · Mastery 10

Direct hits build Depth over ten seconds. One water burn per enemy and caster grows stronger with Depth; extra hits refresh it instead of stacking extra burns. Three seconds without a Sunken hit resets that enemy’s Depth. Protection and restoration retain their original functions.
