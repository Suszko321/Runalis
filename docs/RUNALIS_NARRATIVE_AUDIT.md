# RUNALIS narrative and world assessment

31 October 2026. Initial departmental assessment; existing game source and local authored content inspected. No main-story rewrite, world expansion, new faction system or canon replacement was performed.

The project has a much stronger Act I and physical world than the old July/October audit snapshots imply. The most serious current defect is later-story completion through broad boss matching without prerequisite checks. The largest design gap is the difference between the current Three Seraphs direction and the older implemented Acts II/III. Protect the recent Act I work while addressing those issues.

## Evidence and scope

Read the existing canonical foundation; narrative/world audits and migration reports; current campaign, dialogue, Malakar, Orchard, Grove, Veyreign, Root & Retort, continent, encounter and persistence code; relevant source consumers and regression tools. Indexed all Markdown documents under `docs/`, including headings, in [DOCUMENTS.md](narrative-audit/DOCUMENTS.md) / generated `documents.json`. This index is a location aid; it does not claim every unrelated combat/art document received a semantic lore review. No other story document formats were treated as canonical without reading their contents.

The current executable inventory is generated by [audit-narrative-foundation.mjs](../tools/audit-narrative-foundation.mjs). It constructs the root world with published editor overrides disabled, installs the campaign and Old Well layers in an appropriate review quest state, constructs Orrakai/recovery/prologue, 35 dungeon floors across 11 identities and the authored Grove. It records conditional actors without claiming they are all simultaneously available. All checked giver, talk-NPC, objective-POI, dungeon-entrance, named-enemy and item-definition references resolve. This is reference coverage, not a full quest-completion certificate or proof of every item source.

The prior dirty working tree contained art/combat/multiplayer work and README changes. Those were preserved. New assessment files and tools belong to this task; the historical bible received only a navigation note. The existing Malakar browser verifier received a selector update to the current consent UI. No runtime `src/` files were changed.

## Act I: current sequence and preservation requirements

The [World Bible](RUNALIS_WORLD_BIBLE.md#10-main-story-chronology-and-dependencies) contains the complete stage-ID sequence and dependency diagram. The current route is:

1. Hired caravan guard, Last Dawn ambush, stolen relic, recovery above Root & Retort.
2. Mercy: Mara → Mael → relief/water/watch → Mael → Callworth → Old Well's three enemies and five clues → lantern first-report branch → timed healing rite → joint discussion → Mara's upstairs evening conclusion.
3. Ashes: wreck manifest/cradle → Hessa's explicit assignment → three Orchard clues → actual authored cellar clear → Callworth then Mara → two Chapel witnesses, records and regulator → Keep orders/defector and Mael → four-floor descent.
4. Deliberate Sanctum entry → four leaders sacrifice themselves → ritual/introduction → mandatory Malakar battle → final testimony → physical broken reliquary recovery → Mael/Mara aftermath.

These are implemented improvements, not missing proposals. Key preserved consequences include lantern custody, report/reputation history, remembered questions, Order research/intake choices and POI control. Required Chapel witnesses remain eligible after optional resolution. The distant Hunter Scout is distinct from Hessa at the wreck.

Checks passed for all six classes and both report routes, totaling 12 complete scripted flows and 504 save/load checkpoints, without quest-stage injection. Travel is repositioned and enemy deaths expedited, so this does not certify an uninterrupted manual campaign, normally earned combat equipment, resource balance or every dialogue branch. See [validation](narrative-audit/VALIDATION.md).

## Later acts: implemented content versus approved direction

Act II currently has The Beautiful Cage (`act2AureateReckoning`), three sequential stages, an Elian conversation plus six kills, and Shattered Choir/Seraphel completion. Act III has Sever the Light (`act3Severance`), two generic kill stages, Ascension Spire/Zephyr completion and ending-opportunity flags. Their regions, entrances and enemies physically exist on the continent.

The Three Seraphs is absent as a quest contract. There are no established three artifact identities, independent domain progression, negotiated outcomes or artifact admission condition. Exact Seraph identities and artifact rationale must remain unresolved, not invented during this audit. Continuing to cooperate with the Covenant after Malakar needs an explicit current mission and character acknowledgment when this act is authored.

Zarynth has a functioning arrival/Trade Ward, not a complete capital. Physical roads and a Nav Stone permit early access; the greater northern districts remain closed/unbuilt. The director's all-three-artifacts-before-entry requirement and existing early public access need a deliberate boundary decision. Do not add an invisible continent wall or arbitrarily call the Trade Ward exempt.

## Confirmed high-impact defects and discrepancies

### N-001 — Later acts can complete without their prerequisites

**Confirmed runtime defect, highest repair priority.** `combat.js` → `bossSystem.applyBossDefeatRewards` → `storyRuntime.applyBossStoryProgress` matches Seraphel/Choir and Zephyr/Spire by broad text and records completion even when the quest was never started. `completeOrRecordQuest` fabricates a completed quest record if ordinary completion fails.

Reproduction in the audit tool uses a fresh in-memory player, a real final-floor Shattered Choir or Ascension Spire, sets the actual boss HP to zero and runs the production `updateEnemies` death loop. The resulting character has `act2Complete` or `act3Complete`, completed quest records and later revelation/unlock flags despite no preceding campaign. The world dungeon also clears. A generated optional holy dungeon demonstrates the broad boss-type match hazard; that third case is a controlled factory probe, not a claim that a specific live optional holy entrance currently exposes it. The existing failed-purification side-zone factory demotes its bosses and does not trigger that probe.

This is independent of the passing arrival guard tests. Physical freedom is correct; story-credit eligibility is not. Repair should preserve early exploration and earned world rewards while protecting campaign completion/revelations with stable encounter identity, prerequisites and explicit stages. Reconcile already affected saves deliberately. No runtime repair was made in this initial assessment.

### N-002 — Three Seraphs and Zarynth requirement not implemented

**Confirmed requirement gap.** The old single-Seraphel act conflicts with current director direction. Domain identities, artifact purpose and entry boundary are decisions, not bugs to patch by guessing. The World Bible records confirmed requirements and unresolved choices separately. Existing Act II/III saves and Elian's Choir quest depend on the old progression.

### N-003 — Active later dialogue carries conflicting legacy mythology

`dialogueData.js:lastLanternCrossingDialogue` explicitly says Runalis was first a name/person rather than a world. The canonical foundation defines Runalis as the physical world from the First Keeping. The same Echo frames Zephyr around ending loneliness/grief; the foundation establishes a manufactured messiah who understands manipulation and chooses possession/control. Dusk's later dialogue exposes an old Unbound framing. These are active data, not just dead roadmap comments.

`bossData.js` separately describes Seraphel as a fallen angel rejecting Ascension; it is not part of the active startup graph. Do not conflate dormant contradiction with a witnessed in-game scene. Seraphel is an Avarran Primearch under confirmed canon. Revelation access, invocation sites and player knowledge need focused review before editing later conversations.

### N-004 — Old campaign text remains in saved/registered content

`hollowCrown`/`solgateOath` are suppressed from fresh generic offers, but retained definitions still name the Veyr Warden, Ember Veil and old Solgate sequence. Current castle content is Alistair Veyreign and a mandatory beast transformation; the exterior `lordVeyrShade` identity is now Castellan Edrik's Echo. Some residual side text still addresses the old lord/Unbound premise. Preserve compatibility while deciding how old active tasks should read/resolve; do not revive them as the current spine.

### N-005 — Reputation mostly lacks broader world response

Campaign choices write `factionReputation` and the journal displays it. Current source consumers do not establish general prices/access/hostility/faction-quest thresholds. Keys also differ from registry identities (`covenant` / `covenantOfLight`, `wardens` / `outpost`). Your Order has real separate trust, policy, refusal, assignment and shelter behavior; preserve those consequences. A smaller set of legible responses is preferable to an unplanned diplomacy engine.

### N-006 — Forward consequences have unconsumed promises

Chapel choices change local props, services, witnesses and summaries, which are real outcomes. `future.*` flags such as surviving Rusk and Covenant distrust are authored hooks without a demonstrated later encounter/quest response. A future line in a journal must not be counted as an implemented follow-up. Audit each promise/consumer and scope callbacks before adding new branches.

### N-007 — World lore outpaces political and cultural delivery

Thalassia Republic, Eidralis and Lumora lack the developed governments, borders, diplomatic interests and physical communities needed for their intended significance. Nonhuman peoples exist in canonical mythology, while the playable character/NPC architecture remains largely human plus bloodlines. Zarynth's inner city, political cast and Synod investigation sites are not complete. These are large authored content tasks requiring approved briefs, not a reason to scatter generic NPCs.

### N-008 — Rot requires a coherent documented ecology contract

Orchard, Chapel and Grove already provide specific ecological/supernatural observations. No complete central lifecycle/species/vector contract for Blight Flowers, root reservoirs and Rotflower Beasts was identified. Distinguish engineered modern Rot from other ancient corruption, and treatments from cures. Avoid retroactively making every supernatural grove identical.

### N-009 — Validation baselines and old reports are stale

`verify-world-rebuild-contracts.mjs` currently fails two groups: its frozen Mercy contract predates later approved stages/branches, and it requires a Pilgrim Bridge Nav Stone deliberately removed by `navStoneNetwork.js`. The current campaign/access/persistence/arrival suites pass. Keep the historical baseline as migration evidence; write current contracts and retired-ID expectations rather than reverting working story improvements to satisfy it. `docs/world-rebuild/README.md` also contains outdated claims about Git validity, 64 encounter snapshots and original compact-map counts; current sparse-state verification exercises a 192-snapshot bound.

The Malakar browser verifier initially timed out waiting for the obsolete `#malakarEncounterPanel` readiness UI. Readiness now uses `#partyEntryPanel` and actor-owned vote buttons. This assessment updates only those two test selectors and reruns the same encounter coverage. Story/runtime behavior is unchanged.

## World, discovery and dungeon conclusions

Echoryn has the strongest authored density: geographically consistent roads/waterways, Orchard/Chapel/Keep relationships, optional Grove/Veyreign, resources, camps and clues. Access checks passed 1,045 early interaction targets with the campaign layer, plus 1,122 targets in the independent continental fixture, including 103 later targets. These counts differ because fixtures install different layers. They prove reachable interaction approaches within their scope, not safe combat travel or all visibility states.

The continent provides plausible alternate routes and fixed geographic danger. Large off-road areas and later regions remain comparatively thin; location counts are not a substitute for discovery variety. A sensible next exploration pass should follow an approved regional story brief, use actual rivers/roads/landmarks and attach each reward to a historical or ecological observation. Preserve quiet stretches and current Nav Stone spacing.

Authored dungeon modules demonstrate a good reusable standard: distinctive original purpose, required interactions, place-specific narrative, boss context, consequences and return rules. Many other generated dungeons retain shared room/pack structures and shallow story purpose. Improve selected important sites one at a time; do not replace all generators during a narrative repair.

## Save and multiplayer assessment

Schema 18 preserves quest stages/reward claims, story/POI flags, topic/effect history, sparse continental state and personal transformations. Current campaign migration maps old stage identities and protects old cellar progression. Save/load checks cover interrupted dialogue, ritual/introduction/victory, instance returns and 100 continental hydration cycles. These are substantial protections, not exhaustive compatibility with every old player profile.

Shared campaign progress follows the quest owner; bloodline progression and permanent transformations remain personal. Relevant checks passed for secondary-player conversations/regulator progress, independent topic history, 2–4 player entry consent, reject/timeout/stale-party behavior and scene gathering. Physical controllers, every quest choice across two active humans, every online interaction and long-session timing were not comprehensively played in this assessment. Existing multiplayer department findings remain their owner's responsibility and should be consulted for cross-system work.

## Recommended correction order

1. Repair later-boss prerequisite/identity handling and its save-state consequences; retain open exploration.
2. Reconcile stale validation contracts and active legacy-lore/revelation defects with explicit migration coverage.
3. Obtain the few necessary Act II decisions: trio/domain identities, artifact purpose, Covenant commission and Zarynth admission boundary. Prepare a reviewable spec before runtime changes.
4. Implement The Three Seraphs as complete physical adventures with independent order, consequences and migration; preserve existing companion/Choir dependencies deliberately.
5. Build approved Zarynth political/Synod investigation content and reconcile final-act geography/outcomes before implementing late reveals.
6. Add focused reputation callbacks, Rot ecology delivery, companion reactions and selected regional discoveries using existing systems.

The detailed [backlog](RUNALIS_NARRATIVE_BACKLOG.md) gives acceptance criteria and dependencies. The department is ready for the first focused assignment; this assessment does not assume authorization to decide unresolved main-story outcomes.
