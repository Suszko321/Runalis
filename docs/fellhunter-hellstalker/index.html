A mobile hunter who trades safety for sustained fire. Keep your quarry burning, prepare the finishing shot, then move before the enemy can answer.

Ⅰ · Ignite

Direct hits leave a short 1.8-second Infernal Brand, ticking hard every 0.3 seconds. Spaced pressure builds up to three Embers, strengthening the same burn. A simultaneous volley cannot instantly build all three.

Ⅱ · Hunt

Hits grant brief Cinder Pace. Hook forward, vault backward, or lay fire-jaws and ember caltrops. Solid terrain still stops your movement and shots.

Ⅲ · Flashover

Marked Shot, Bomb Toss and Deadfall consume a prepared three-Ember brand for a heavier ignition. Casting leaves you taking 10% more damage for five seconds.
