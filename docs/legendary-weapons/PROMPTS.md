# Legendary weapon art — accepted prompts

Built-in ImageGen; six accepted transparent masters. Native exports are mechanical palette reduction, scaling and grip registration, not programmatic weapon drawing. Original player/weapon assets are preserved. One sword master with clipped tips was rejected and regenerated. Actual transparent gutters, rather than assumed equal column widths, isolate complete drawings.

Source masters are saved locally under `assets/art/source/legendary-weapons/` (ignored authoring files). Runtime sprites and metadata are under `assets/art/sprites/legendary-weapon-*` and `legendary-focus-projectiles.*`. Reproduce exports with `tools/pack-legendary-weapons.py`. Preview evidence is local in `docs/legendary-weapons/`.
