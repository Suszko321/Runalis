❄ Cryomancer

Shape ice armor, piercing icicles and blizzards. Prepare an enemy with frost, then break it with a decisive strike. Cryomancer is Waverider’s control and prepared-burst family, available at Mastery 20; previously earned unlocks remain usable.

1 · Build Chill2 · Freeze / Brittle3 · Shatter

Three spaced Chill applications make a target Brittle. Lesser foes freeze briefly; bosses remain mobile. Ice blades, Pressure Spear and Deluge Crown consume Brittle for 40% more damage before other bonuses, with a small shard burst. Other frost hits gain 10% while Brittle lasts. Freeze has recovery time, and cannot interrupt elite modifier effects.

Read the target

Small frost marks show Chill. Ice encases a frozen enemy; the pale snowflake identifies Brittle.

Release Shatter

Cryomancer trades some sustained damage for control, armor and timed bursts. Sunken remains the stronger sustained burner in long fights. Original skills retain Living Water’s rhythm.
