# Endgame equipment — accepted art prompts

Built-in ImageGen, November 1, 2026. Twelve new masters; transparent backgrounds requested. Supporting reference `assets/art/sprites/player-wearables-covenant.png` supplies native pixel language, while the new request supplies the ornate high-fantasy design direction. Original player/body sprite files are read-only. No CLI/API fallback used.

All accepted masters were inspected and copied to `assets/art/source/endgame-equipment/<sheet>-master.png` (local ignored authoring sources). `tools/pack-endgame-equipment.py` measures actual transparent row gutters, isolates each item, quantizes to 24 colours per view and packs native registrations plus 32px inventory icons. Runtime PNG/JSON exports live in `assets/art/sprites/` and are tracked. The four rows are helm, chest, legs and mantle; columns front/right/back/left. No human anatomy or walking frames were generated.

## Starweaver

Master: `assets/art/source/endgame-equipment/endgame-starweaver-master.png`

Runtime: `assets/art/sprites/endgame-starweaver.png` and `endgame-starweaver-icons.png`, with JSON contracts.

Use case: stylized-concept. RUNALIS production high-fantasy ENDGAME equipment pixel-art sheet. Supporting reference defines the game's deliberate pixel clusters and dark outlines, but this new gear must look spectacular and prestigious: rich jewel colours, elegant metal filigree, sculpted crests, intricate but readable heraldry and dramatic mantles. Transparent background, no character, mannequin, skin, face or body, no text, labels, scenery or weapons. Exactly FOUR columns front/right/back/left, and FOUR rows. Every item isolated with generous transparent gutters. Row1 ornate mage headpiece, transparent face/eye aperture where appropriate, compact wearable proportions with an impressive crest; row2 isolated fitted torso armour/vestment only, no arms, hands or skirt; row3 fitted paired leg armour/boots over trousers, no exaggerated pelvis; row4 isolated long shoulder-to-ankle mantle with open front, narrow side view and heraldic back. Each view consistent with its row's design. Crisp low-resolution sprite clusters, 24-colour ramps, ink borders and jewel highlights, no blurry painting or external glow, no particles. Readable at native helm30x28, torso18x18, legs26x30, mantle40x54. Tall crests and ornate cloak hems give a distinctive silhouette without redesigning the body. Ensemble: Starweaver, legendary mage. Midnight indigo silk, silver lunar filigree and lilac crystals; tall split arcane hood, layered star-embroidered cuirass vest, silver-bound fitted boots, broad crescent-edged indigo mantle. Matching materials and emblem across all four pieces.
