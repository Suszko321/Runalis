SUNKEN · MASTERY 5

Build the pressure.

Midnight-blue water builds shared Depth over twenty seconds. Carry that power between foes; a killing blow preserves it for the next encounter contact.

One burn per target and caster. Three-second burn tails; five seconds without a hit resets Depth. Healing and pressure burns cannot build the ramp.

CRYOMANCER · MASTERY 10

Prepare. Freeze. Shatter.

Space your Chill hits to make targets Brittle, then break them with an ice blade or lance. Lesser enemies freeze briefly; bosses resist immobilization.

Crystal wards protect the party. Restorative Rime and Glacial Sanctuary heal and clear movement slows and accumulated elite frost.

Depth becomes visible.

The blue player-frame meter tracks the shared ramp. A fresh target inherits built Depth while pressure continues.

Five seconds without a qualifying hit resets Depth; each pressure burn lasts up to three seconds after its last source hit.

TWELVE FROST TECHNIQUES

Cryomancer

Slows, brief freezes, prepared Shatter hits, movement, wards, healing and restorative utility.

Whiteout

Place a broad, five-second blizzard in the aimed area. Snow and ice rake enemies inside, building Chill and Brittle. Pay Spirit once; the field does not follow you.

Try this skill ↗

Permafrost

Lay an aimed patch of jagged frost for four seconds. Its light damage and repeated Chill slow approaching enemies and prepare them for Shatter.

Try this skill ↗

Glacial Lance

Fire a long piercing icicle through lined-up enemies. Consume Brittle for 50% more damage before bonuses and a small shard burst. Charge it for a stronger prepared strike.

Try this skill ↗

Frost Mirror

Raise a four-second frost mirror that reduces damage by 22% before bonuses. Attackers receive a bounded ice reply that builds Chill. Retaliation has a shared cooldown.

Try this skill ↗

Frost Shard

Launch a fast ice shard at one enemy. Hits build Chill; three spaced hits make the target Brittle and briefly freeze weaker foes.

Try this skill ↗

Crystal Aegis

Wrap yourself and nearby allies in crystal armor for four seconds, reducing incoming damage by 24% before bonuses. This protects allies without freezing nearby enemies for free.

Try this skill ↗

Restorative Rime

Send restorative frost through you and nearby allies. Restore health, clear movement slows and accumulated elite frost, then grant a modest two-second ice ward. Does not cleanse poison or fire.

Try this skill ↗

Shattering Blade

Drive a piercing ice blade through lined-up enemies. Against a Brittle target, consume Brittle for a stronger Shatter hit. Otherwise build Chill.

Try this skill ↗

Glacial Glide

Glide forward over passable ground and leave a short frost patch at your departure. Crossed enemies and foes standing in the patch build Chill; solid walls stop the glide.

Try this skill ↗

Icebound Spires

Raise ice spires in an aimed circle for nearly five seconds. Repeated pulses build Chill toward a brief freeze and Brittle; entering the field is not an instant freeze.

Try this skill ↗

Glacial Sanctuary

Plant a five-second glacial sanctuary at your feet. Each second, allies inside recover health and shed movement slows and accumulated elite frost. Enemies take light frost damage and build Chill. Healing zones from this skill do not stack.

Try this skill ↗

Crown of Winter

Erupt a crown of ice in an aimed area. Brittle enemies take a powerful Shatter hit; other enemies build Chill. Nearby allies gain a brief ice ward. Best after preparing targets with frost.
